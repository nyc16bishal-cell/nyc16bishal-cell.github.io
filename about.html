Background

Who I am

I'm Bishal Gupta, a full-stack software developer with experience spanning enterprise Java backends, modern React and Angular frontends, and data engineering. My career has taken me from data analysis at a financial services firm to building production systems in a Google engineering environment.

I hold a Master's in Data Science alongside a background in Computer Science and Computer Information Systems — a blend that keeps me equally comfortable reasoning about data pipelines and shipping polished user-facing features.

I write code that my future self (and teammates) can still understand six months later. That means meaningful tests, clear naming, and avoiding cleverness for its own sake.
