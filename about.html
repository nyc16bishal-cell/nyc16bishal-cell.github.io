Background

Who I am

I'm Bishal Gupta, a software engineer and data analyst with 5+ years of experience building scalable web applications and data-driven solutions. My career has taken me from data analysis at a financial services firm to building production interfaces in a Google engineering environment.

I'm currently pursuing a Master's in Data Science at Saint Peter's University with a 4.0 GPA, building on a Computer Science degree from Saint Joseph University and an Associate's in Computer Information Systems from ASA College — a blend that keeps me equally comfortable reasoning about data pipelines and shipping polished user-facing features.

I write code that my future self (and teammates) can still understand six months later. That means meaningful tests, clear naming, and avoiding cleverness for its own sake.
