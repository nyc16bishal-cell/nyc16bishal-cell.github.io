Perspective

Theory meets practice.

The Data Science master's isn't just a credential — it actively shapes how I approach engineering. When a query is slow, I think about cardinality and distribution. When designing a feature, I ask what we're measuring and why. That quantitative lens is rare in a pure software engineer and genuinely useful in product conversations.

The CS bachelor's gave me the language of correctness — Big-O, invariants, soundness — while the CIS associates anchored it in real organisational contexts. Together they mean I can reason from first principles and still keep a pragmatic eye on what actually ships.
